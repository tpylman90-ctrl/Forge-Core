# Forge Seed v0.7 — Question-answer apprenticeship experiment

## Why

The original scratch 2.22M-parameter model improved dramatically at short
story generation after 14,000 pretraining steps, but its separate simple
understanding benchmark returned **19/40 (47.5%)** on mirrored A/B choices,
approximately chance-level. Its story-language ability should be preserved.

The first task is a supervised, **gold-verified curriculum** for reading
a short statement and selecting an answer. This is not personal "ask why"
learning, and all labels are generated by deterministic rules.

## Controlled experiment

Three groups share exactly the same original Forge Seed checkpoint and
tokenizer: frozen baseline; verified-answer adaptation; class-balanced
shuffled-answer adaptation. The two adapted groups share exactly the same
questions, order, AdamW step count, fixed seed and public TinyStories next-
token rehearsal. Only the answer labels are swapped.

The training generator creates 1,600 original labeled examples across:
color; who received an object; spatial location; number. A/B positions
are balanced per skill. Evaluation uses both the original **40-choice**
paired preschool assessment and a separately phrased **40-choice transfer**
assessment with distinct people; the two answer-choice mirrors each refer
to one underlying story, so neither is 40 independent cases.

Target: improve **understanding** without destroying story generation.
Measure accuracy by skill, mirrored-choice consistency, held-out next-
token loss on the original validation corpus and fixed-seed story samples.

## Guardrails

- Training and evaluation examples use disjoint people. Training language
  templates differ from evaluation but underlying relations overlap.
- Source includes only properly attributed public TinyStories and original
  programmatically verified synthetic examples. No user memories, emails
  or observation data.
- Existing v0.7 checkpoint and historical v0.6 checkpoint remain unchanged.
  Both new candidate weights are marked **experimental only**.
- A high A/B score alone is not fluent language, real conversation, causal
  understanding or an independently verified preschool capability.
- To promote, we need consistency on new construction types, multiple seeds,
  unchanged general-language ability and open-ended instruction evaluation.
- Results that show only prompt-template learning or accuracy near chance
  will be recorded as failures and used to design the next small experiment.
