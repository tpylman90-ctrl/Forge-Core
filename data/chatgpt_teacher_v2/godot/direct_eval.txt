Independent held-out direct-v2 examples.

Task: A PackedScene resource is loaded but no actor appears in the running game.
Procedure:
1. Call instantiate() to create the node hierarchy.
2. Call add_child() under the correct parent.
3. Inspect the running Remote scene tree.
Principle: Loading, instantiation, and insertion into the active tree are separate steps.
Check: Confirm the actor appears under the expected parent.

Task: A button stops responding after a scene refactor.
Procedure:
1. Check that the pressed signal remains connected.
2. Confirm the receiver node and callback still exist.
3. Run the scene and observe the callback.
Principle: Visible controls require a valid event connection to perform behavior.
Check: Confirm one click calls the expected method exactly once.
