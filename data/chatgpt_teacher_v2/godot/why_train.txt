Case: A CharacterBody3D slides through a wall at some frame rates.
Forge first attempt: Lower speed until it stops clipping.
ChatGPT asks: Is movement done in the fixed physics callback, and are collisions handled through the body movement method?
Forge diagnoses: I treated a symptom by tuning speed before checking the update path.
ChatGPT explains:
1. Render-frame timing can vary.
2. Physics callbacks use a fixed step.
3. Collision-aware body movement should use the engine movement path and valid collision shapes.
Analogy: A metronome gives steady beats even when the screen refresh changes.
Principle: Check update timing and collision setup before reducing intended speed.
Forge teach-back: Use physics timing for physical motion, then verify shape and layers.
Faded retrieval: Name two checks before tuning speed.
Forge retrieves: Check _physics_process/move_and_slide and collision shape, layer, and mask configuration.
Transfer challenge: A hover racer moves smoothly but enters an Area3D without a signal. What do you inspect?
Forge transfer: Check monitoring, masks, shapes, and signal connection; do not change vehicle speed.
Forge checks: Reproduce with a minimal scene and confirm the expected signal.

Case: A scene instance appears at an unexpected location after its parent node is rotated.
Forge first attempt: Add the desired world coordinates directly to position.
ChatGPT asks: Is the desired point parent-relative or world-relative?
Forge diagnoses: I used local position as if it were global.
ChatGPT explains:
1. A node's position is relative to its parent.
2. Parent transforms affect the world position.
3. Use the coordinate-space conversion that matches the goal.
Analogy: One step forward inside a rotating room differs from one step north on a map.
Principle: Name the coordinate space before writing a transform.
Forge teach-back: Use local position for parent-relative layout and global coordinates for world placement.
Faded retrieval: How would you test this under a rotated parent?
Forge retrieves: Compare position and global_position in a minimal rotated-parent scene.
Transfer challenge: Place a waypoint at a world-space race gate from a child node.
Forge transfer: Convert the world point to the appropriate local space or assign the correct global transform.
Forge checks: Rotate the parent and confirm the waypoint remains at the intended world location.
Confidence: Confirm the node type and Godot version when using transform APIs.

Case: A reusable health component directly updates a HUD node by a hard-coded path.
Forge first attempt: Add another node path for every UI listener.
ChatGPT asks: Should the health component know which systems respond to health changes?
Forge diagnoses: I coupled the data owner to every possible consumer.
ChatGPT explains:
1. The component can emit a typed signal when health changes.
2. UI, sound, and game logic can connect independently.
3. Test that the signal fires once with the expected value.
Analogy: A doorbell announces a visitor without knowing every person in the house.
Principle: Use events to reduce unnecessary dependencies.
Forge teach-back: The emitter reports; listeners choose their response.
Faded retrieval: Give one situation where a direct call is still simpler.
Forge retrieves: Direct calls are reasonable for a stable, deliberate dependency; signals help when multiple independent listeners may react.
Transfer challenge: A race checkpoint should notify audio and UI without depending on either. What pattern fits?
Forge transfer: Emit a typed checkpoint event and connect audio and UI listeners.
Forge checks: Remove one listener and confirm the checkpoint still works.
