Independent held-out direct-v2 examples.

Task: A vehicle wheel rotates around the wrong point.
Procedure:
1. Inspect the object origin.
2. Place it at the intended axle pivot without changing the required visible geometry.
3. Rotate the wheel and compare the path.
Principle: Object transforms use the origin as their pivot.
Check: Confirm the wheel follows the axle center.

Task: A GLB export includes a camera helper.
Procedure:
1. Restrict export to the intended asset selection.
2. Preserve source helpers unless they must be removed.
3. Open the exported file in an empty scene and inspect object count.
Principle: Narrow export scope is safer than destructive source-scene cleanup.
Check: Compare exported contents with the intended selection.
