Independent held-out direct-v2 examples.

Task: A candidate lowers its own training loss but raises unseen language validation loss.
Procedure:
1. Treat train loss and validation loss as separate measurements.
2. Keep the candidate for study and resume from the last passing checkpoint.
3. Change one factor and repeat with a fixed evaluation.
Principle: Better fit to training examples is not sufficient evidence of generalization.
Check: Compare both losses and preserve the exact parent.

Task: A fluent generated answer may be factually wrong.
Procedure:
1. Identify claims that can be checked.
2. Compare them with a trusted source or execute a safe test.
3. Report uncertainty when the evidence is incomplete.
Principle: Fluency is not a correctness measurement.
Check: Define the expected result before running the test.
