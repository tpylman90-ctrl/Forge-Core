### New experiment

Use strictly disjoint training/development/exam names, objects and
grammar templates; truth must be independently parser-reconstructed.
Extract candidate names without looking at gold. Natural question text
conditions the neural ranking head. Train one correct-label arm and one
within-role-shuffled-label arm, same architecture, initial weights,
batch selection and optimization budget (350 steps each).

Evaluate the no-training baseline, both arms and prior pointer with a
restricted two-name candidate set. Report individual exact-match,
both giver/recipient questions correct, both counterfactual swaps
correct, and per-template/per-role splits. Do not optimize on the
final synthetic exam; treat it as one-time diagnostic evidence, not
a preschool qualification.

No pretrained language-model weights, personal memory, Android data or
external tool action may enter this experiment. Research-only weights,
metrics and SHA256 lineage can be saved as temporary GitHub artifacts.
