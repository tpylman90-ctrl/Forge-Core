# Forge Core — Founding Charter v2.0

Approved direction: November 25, 2026. Supersedes the personal-assistant charter v1.0, preserved in Git history and the [archive branch](https://github.com/tpylman90-ctrl/Forge-Core/tree/archive/pre-open-3d-2026-11-25).

> If we genuinely want technology to benefit humanity, we shouldn't just democratize access to finished technology. We should democratize the ability to create it.

**Mission:** To democratize professional-quality 3D creation by developing free, open and accessible intelligent modeling tools that empower people to transform their ideas into reality, regardless of their education, technical experience or financial resources.

## Commitments

1. Accessibility: design for creators with no formal modeling training, including phone-only users.
2. Affordability: prioritize free tools, local execution and realistic consumer-hardware requirements.
3. Openness: pursue open-source code, documented reproducible research and legally redistributable weights and assets.
4. Quality: measure geometry, editability, texture quality, performance and interoperability; never confuse a proxy score with production quality.
5. Collaboration: specialize in 3D production and complement existing AI assistants; avoid general-purpose assistant replication.
6. Ownership and consent: keep creator files under creator control; no private references in public datasets without explicit permission; external actions require scoped authorization.
7. Transparency: preserve negative results, provenance, checkpoints and independent evaluations.
8. Community: document interfaces and welcome contributions regardless of background.

## Scope and governance

Blender first; independent AI-native geometry backend later. Active research is geometry, surfaces, topology, materials, inspection and export. Prior language, reasoning and personal-memory work is historical, not an active training objective. Keep its history intact. Model candidates are never automatically promoted. The project owner approves charter changes. No official affiliation with any AI provider is implied.
